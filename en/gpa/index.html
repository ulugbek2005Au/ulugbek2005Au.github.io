What Is GPA?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It summarizes academic performance using numerical grade points. Many universities use a 4.0 scale, although grading systems differ between institutions.

How to Calculate Weighted GPA

Multiply each course's grade points by its credits. Add those products together, then divide by the total number of credits.

GPA = Sum of (Grade Points × Credits) / Total Credits

GPA Calculation Example

Suppose you completed three courses:

  • Course 1: 4.0 grade points, 3 credits
  • Course 2: 3.0 grade points, 4 credits
  • Course 3: 3.5 grade points, 2 credits

Weighted points: (4.0 × 3) + (3.0 × 4) + (3.5 × 2) = 31

Total credits: 3 + 4 + 2 = 9

GPA = 31 / 9 = 3.44

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this GPA calculator free?

Yes. You can calculate your GPA without registration or payment.

Does every university use a 4.0 GPA scale?

No. Some universities use different scales or conversion methods. Check your institution's grading policy before using the result.

Why are credits important?

Credits determine the weight of each course. A course with more credits contributes more to your weighted GPA.

Can I calculate GPA for multiple courses?

Yes. Click Add course to enter additional grade points and credits.

Is this my official university GPA?

Not necessarily. Your university may apply additional rules, such as grade conversions or repeated-course policies.
