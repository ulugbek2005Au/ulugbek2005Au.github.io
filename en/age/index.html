What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is a tool that determines how much time has passed since your date of birth. It helps you find your age in completed years, months and days.

Unlike simply subtracting birth years, a calendar-based calculation considers whether your birthday has occurred during the current year.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Choose your date of birth.
  2. Click the Calculate Age button.
  3. Read your age in years, months and days.
  4. Click Reset to start again.

How Is Age Calculated?

Age is calculated by comparing the date of birth with today's date. The calculator counts completed calendar years, then the remaining months and days.

Simple Age Calculation Example

Suppose someone was born on January 1, 2000. On January 1, 2026, that person is exactly 26 years old.

If the current date is before their birthday, they have not yet completed the next full year of age.

When Is an Age Calculator Useful?

Age calculators can be useful for birthdays, personal planning, school applications and checking age requirements.

For official legal or administrative purposes, verify the result against the applicable rules and documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this age calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is free to use without creating an account.

Can I calculate my age in months and days?

Yes. The result includes completed years, remaining months and days.

Does the calculator use today's date?

Yes. It uses the current date from your device.

Can I enter a future birth date?

No. Future birth dates are rejected as invalid.

Does the calculator store my birthday?

The calculation runs in your browser. This tool does not need to send your birth date to a server.

Does it account for leap years?

The calculator uses calendar dates, including leap-year dates. For dates near the end of a month, results may depend on the convention used to express remaining months and days.
