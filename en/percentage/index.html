What Is a Percentage?

A percentage expresses a number as a fraction of 100. Percentages are commonly used for grades, statistics, financial comparisons and everyday calculations.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To find what percentage a part represents of a whole, divide the part by the whole and multiply the result by 100.

Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

Example 1: Test Score

If you answered 45 questions correctly out of 60 questions:

(45 / 60) × 100 = 75%

Example 2: Budget

If you spend $250 out of a $1,000 budget:

(250 / 1000) × 100 = 25%

Can a percentage be greater than 100%?

Yes. A percentage exceeds 100% when the part is larger than the whole, assuming the whole is positive.

What happens if the whole is zero?
