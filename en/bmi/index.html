What Is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a screening measure that compares a person's weight with their height. It is commonly used to classify weight categories in adults, but it does not directly measure body fat.

How to Calculate BMI

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)²

For example, if someone weighs 75 kg and is 1.80 meters tall:

BMI = 75 / (1.80 × 1.80) = 23.1

Adult BMI Categories

The following standard BMI categories are commonly used for adults:

  • Underweight: Below 18.5
  • Healthy weight: 18.5–24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0–29.9
  • Obesity: 30.0 or higher

Limitations of BMI

BMI is not a medical diagnosis. It does not distinguish muscle from fat or account for differences in body composition. It may be less informative for athletes, pregnant people and some other groups.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this BMI calculator free?

Yes. You can calculate your BMI without registering or paying.

Does BMI measure body fat directly?

No. BMI is an indirect screening measure based on weight and height.

Can children use adult BMI categories?

No. Children and teenagers require age- and sex-specific BMI interpretation. This calculator is intended for adults.

Is a normal BMI a guarantee of good health?

No. BMI alone cannot determine a person's overall health. Other health indicators and individual circumstances also matter.
