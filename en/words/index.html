What Is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online tool that helps you measure the number of words and characters in a piece of text. It can be useful for essays, articles, assignments, blog posts and other writing tasks.

How to Use This Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. View the word and character counts.
  3. Check the estimated reading time.
  4. Click Reset to clear your text.

How Is Reading Time Calculated?

Estimated reading time is based on a reading speed of approximately 200 words per minute. Actual reading speed varies depending on the reader and the complexity of the text.

Reading time (minutes) = Word count / 200

Reading Time Example

A text containing 1,000 words takes approximately 5 minutes to read at 200 words per minute.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this word counter free?

Yes. This word counter is free to use and does not require registration.

Does it count characters?

Yes. The tool displays a character count alongside the word count.

How are words counted?

Words are identified using whitespace separation. This works well for many languages, but languages that do not normally separate words with spaces may require a different counting method.

Does the word count update automatically?

Yes. The counts update as you type or change the text.

Can I use it for essays and assignments?

Yes. It can help you monitor the length of essays, reports and assignments. Always check your institution's specific word-count rules.
